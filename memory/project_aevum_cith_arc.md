After Aevum's deal with the Arke twins and his transfer into Dulla's body, he wanted to investigate the Primals — a civilization Orians and Primals had never directly known. Their histories nearly parallel each other but the two peoples never made contact. He used the twins' order to strike against the Primals as cover for his own research.

**Key arc beats:**
- Aevum (as Dulla) met **Cith More** during this period — using the assault as access to learn from the inside
- Mana had told Cith everything he went through (his binding, the Absolute Rebirth, Cycla's recorded notes)
- Cith became Aevum's entry point into Primal knowledge and Cycla's written record of the Mana binding
- **Discovering Cycla's notes on Mana's binding is what truly set Aevum on sealing the twins** — he found theoretical proof it was possible and the mechanism for how
- Planned reveal: Aevum had **Gaia and Cronus's cores since the events of the Fest** — this is a major late-arc reveal

**Why:** Gives Aevum's betrayal of the twins emotional and intellectual grounding — it wasn't the deal itself that turned him, it was what he learned from Cith about what Mana's fate proved was possible.

**How to apply:** When writing Aevum's arc, Cith More scenes, and the backstory of how the Noble Treasure weapon project began — Cith is the source, not just Cycla's record.
